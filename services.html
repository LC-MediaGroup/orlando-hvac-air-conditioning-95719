AC Repair Orlando

AC Repair

When your air conditioner quits in Florida heat, you need a fix — not a runaround. Our techs diagnose the real problem fast, explain it in plain English, and repair it right the first time, including emergency AC repair Orlando homeowners call us for after hours.

  • Not cooling, blowing warm, or short-cycling
  • Strange noises, smells, or leaking water
  • Frozen coils, tripped breakers, or dead compressors
  • Same-day and emergency AC repair Orlando-wide

How much does AC repair cost?

Most repairs run $150–$650 depending on the part and unit. We diagnose first and give you a flat price before any work starts — no guessing.

How fast can someone come out?

Same-day service is available most days, and we offer emergency AC repair in Orlando for nights and weekends when the heat can't wait.

Do I really need a repair, or can it wait?

Warm air, ice on the lines, rising bills, or a unit that keeps shutting off are all signs to call now before a small issue becomes a full breakdown.

AC Installation

Whether your system is beyond repair or you're upgrading for better efficiency, we handle air conditioning installation in Orlando from sizing to setup — matched to your home, not a one-size-fits-all box.

  • Correctly sized systems for Central Florida homes
  • Energy-efficient units that lower monthly bills
  • Full removal of your old unit, no mess left behind
  • Manufacturer warranties plus our workmanship guarantee

How much does a new AC installation cost?

Pricing depends on system size and efficiency, typically $4,500–$9,500 installed. We give you a firm, written quote before you commit to anything.

How long does installation take?

Most full installs are completed in a single day, so you're not without cool air any longer than necessary.

How do I know I need a replacement, not a repair?

If your unit is 12+ years old, needs frequent repairs, or uses R-22 refrigerant, replacement usually saves more money long-term.

AC Maintenance Orlando FL

AC Maintenance

Routine AC maintenance in Orlando FL keeps small issues from becoming expensive breakdowns during our hottest months. Our tune-ups cover the full system, inside and out, so your AC runs efficiently all year.

  • Coil cleaning, refrigerant check, and electrical inspection
  • Lower energy bills and fewer surprise breakdowns
  • Extends the life of your system by years
  • Keeps manufacturer warranties valid

How much does maintenance cost?

Standard tune-ups start around $89, with discounted annual maintenance plans available for year-round coverage.

How often should I schedule maintenance?

We recommend twice a year in Florida — once before summer, once before winter — to keep your system running at peak performance.

What signs mean I'm overdue?

Rising energy bills, weak airflow, musty smells, or a unit that runs constantly are all signs a tune-up is overdue.
